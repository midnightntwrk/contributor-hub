
# Getting NIGHT Tokens

This guide will walk you through obtaining NIGHT tokens, whether you're getting them directly from exchanges, bridging from other chains, or funding your wallet on the Mainnet.

---

## 1. Where to Get NIGHT Tokens

### Exchanges
NIGHT tokens are available on several major cryptocurrency exchanges. Here are some popular options:

- **Binance**: [NIGHT/BUSD trading pair](https://www.binance.com/en/trade/NIGHT_BUSD)
- **CoinGecko**: [NIGHT token page](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/night)
- **CoinMarketCap**: [NIGHT token page](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/night/)

**Steps to buy NIGHT on Binance:**
1. Create an account on [Binance](https://www.binance.com/)
2. Complete KYC verification if required
3. Deposit funds to your Binance account
4. Navigate to the "Trade" section
5. Select the NIGHT/BUSD trading pair
6. Place your buy order

---

## 2. Bridging NIGHT from Other Chains

If you already have NIGHT tokens on another blockchain, you can bridge them to the Midnight Network Mainnet.

### Using LayerZero Bridge
1. Visit the [LayerZero Bridge](https://layerzero.network/bridge)
2. Select Midnight Network as the destination chain
3. Choose your source chain and select NIGHT as the token
4. Connect your wallet and follow the bridging instructions

### Using Cross-Chain Aggregators
- **THORChain**: [THORChain Bridge](https://thorchain.org/)
- **Wormhole**: [Wormhole Bridge](https://wormhole.com/)

**Steps for THORChain:**
1. Visit [THORChain Bridge](https://thorchain.org/)
2. Select Midnight Network as the destination
3. Choose your source chain and NIGHT token
4. Connect your wallet and complete the bridging process

---

## 3. Funding Your Wallet on Mainnet

Once you have NIGHT tokens, you'll need to fund your wallet on the Midnight Network Mainnet.

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Set Up a Wallet**
   - Install a wallet that supports Midnight Network, such as:
     - [Keplr Wallet](https://wallet.keplr.app/)
     - [Cosmostation](https://www.cosmostation.io/)
     - [Leap Wallet](https://leapwallet.app/)

2. **Connect Your Wallet**
   - Open your wallet app
   - Create a new wallet or import your existing one
   - Connect to the Midnight Network Mainnet

3. **Transfer Tokens**
   - If you bridged tokens, they should appear in your wallet automatically
   - If you bought tokens on an exchange, you'll need to transfer them to your wallet address:
     1. Get your wallet address from the wallet app
     2. On the exchange, initiate a withdrawal to this address
     3. Confirm the transaction

4. **Verify Your Balance**
   - Check your wallet to confirm the tokens have arrived
   - You can also verify on the [Midnight Network Explorer](https://explorer.midnight.network/)

---

## 4. Important Security Tips

- Always double-check wallet addresses before sending tokens
- Use hardware wallets for large amounts
- Keep your private keys secure
- Be cautious of phishing scams
- Only use official bridge and exchange platforms

---

## 5. Troubleshooting

**Issue: Tokens not appearing in wallet**
- Check if the transaction was successful on the explorer
- Ensure you're using the correct network (Midnight Network Mainnet)
- Wait for confirmation (usually 1-5 minutes)

**Issue: Bridging failed**
- Check gas fees and adjust if necessary
- Verify you have enough balance for the bridge transaction
- Contact support if the issue persists

**Issue: Exchange withdrawal issues**
- Contact exchange support with your transaction details
- Ensure you've completed all KYC requirements
- Check for any withdrawal limits or holds
