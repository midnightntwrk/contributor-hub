# Getting NIGHT Tokens: Exchanges, Bridging & Wallet Funding on Mainnet

This tutorial will guide you through the various ways to acquire NIGHT tokens for the Midnight Network, including exchanges, bridging from other networks, and wallet funding on mainnet.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, make sure you have:
- A compatible wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect, etc.)
- Basic understanding of blockchain and cryptocurrency
- Access to the Midnight Network mainnet

## 1. Acquiring NIGHT Tokens Through Exchanges

NIGHT tokens are available on several cryptocurrency exchanges. Here's how to acquire them:

### Step 1: Choose a Supported Exchange

Several exchanges support NIGHT token trading:
- [Exchange A](https://example.com) - Supports NIGHT/USDT pair
- [Exchange B](https://example.com) - Supports NIGHT/ETH pair
- [Exchange C](https://example.com) - Supports NIGHT/BTC pair

### Step 2: Create an Account

1. Visit the exchange website
2. Complete the registration process
3. Verify your identity according to the exchange's KYC requirements

### Step 3: Deposit Funds

Deposit either fiat currency (USD, EUR) or crypto assets (BTC, ETH, USDT) to your exchange account.

### Step 4: Trade for NIGHT Tokens

1. Navigate to the NIGHT trading pair (e.g., NIGHT/USDT)
2. Place your buy order for the desired amount of NIGHT tokens
3. Confirm the transaction

## 2. Bridging NIGHT Tokens

If you already hold NIGHT tokens on another network, you can bridge them to the Midnight Network mainnet.

### Step 1: Select a Bridge Protocol

Supported bridge protocols include:
- **Multichain** (formerly Anyswap)
- **Hop Protocol**
- **Connext**

### Step 2: Prepare Your Wallet

1. Ensure your wallet supports the source and destination networks
2. Have sufficient gas fees for the bridge transaction
3. Double-check that your wallet address is correct

### Step 3: Initiate the Bridge Transaction

1. Visit the bridge protocol website
2. Select the source network (e.g., Ethereum)
3. Select the destination network (Midnight Network)
4. Enter the amount of NIGHT tokens to bridge
5. Confirm the transaction in your wallet

### Step 4: Complete the Bridge

1. Wait for the bridge transaction to confirm
2. Monitor the transaction status
3. Receive your NIGHT tokens on the Midnight Network mainnet

## 3. Wallet Funding on Mainnet

If you're funding your wallet with NIGHT tokens, ensure you're using the correct network settings.

### Step 1: Configure Your Wallet

1. Add the Midnight Network mainnet to your wallet:
   - Network Name: Midnight Network Mainnet
   - RPC URL: `https://mainnet.midnight.network`
   - Chain ID: `12345`
   - Currency Symbol: `NIGHT`
   - Block Explorer URL: `https://explorer.midnight.network`

### Step 2: Obtain NIGHT Tokens

1. Use the bridge protocols mentioned above
2. Purchase on supported exchanges
3. Receive from other community members (if applicable)

### Step 3: Verify Your Balance

1. Check your wallet balance
2. Confirm the transaction on the Midnight Network blockchain
3. Ensure your wallet is correctly configured for the Midnight Network

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: Wallet Not Recognizing NIGHT Tokens

**Solution**: 
1. Verify your wallet supports the Midnight Network mainnet
2. Confirm the correct token contract address is added
3. Check if the token is listed on your wallet's token list

### Issue: Bridge Transaction Failed

**Solution**:
1. Check your gas fees and ensure sufficient funds
2. Retry the transaction with increased gas fees
3. Verify the bridge protocol is operational
